Sponge Cake Recipe

A Sponge Chocolate Cake is a light, airy, and fluffy dessert made with simple ingredients like flour, sugar, eggs, and cocoa powder. It is celebrated for its soft texture, achieved through careful folding of ingredients to retain air in the batter.

Preparation and Cooking Time

  • Prep time: 20minutes
  • Cook time: 30minutes
  • Total time: 50mintues

Servings and Yield

  • Servings: 8 people
  • Yield: One 8-inch round cake

Ingredients

For the cake:

  • 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup (30g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 4 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup (60ml) whole milk
  • 1/4 cup (60ml) vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and line an 8-inch round cake pan.
  2. Sift together the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, beat eggs and sugar with an electric mixer until pale and fluffy (about 5 minutes).
  4. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the egg mixture in batches, alternating with milk and oil.
  5. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  8. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
